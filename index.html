Gravity Ball privacy policy

Effective November 25, 2026

Gameplay data · Online services · Request account or data deletion

Gameplay on your device

Gravity Ball saves your progress, settings, best scores and times, replay traces, earned gold, and cosmetic collection on your device. Tilt controls use motion readings for steering and calibration. Your optional local nickname changes the name you see on your device.

You can play offline. To erase the local save, open Android Settings, select Apps, Gravity Ball, Storage, then Clear storage. This deletes local progress and purchases made with earned gold. Android can restore a previous save when you reinstall, depending on your backup settings. The current Android release candidate limits its backup to gameplay save files. Online sign-in state, pending score uploads, your local nickname and touch-stick side stay on the original device. Earlier test builds used Android’s default backup rules. You can manage Android backups in your device or Google account settings.

Sign-in and rankings

The current Android test release saves scores on your device and skips online sign-in and score queuing. In builds with online rankings, the game connects to Google Play Games at startup. Google provides a game player ID, display name and sign-in authorization code. Unity Authentication uses the code to identify the player and assigns a Unity player ID. The game uses the Play Games display name to set the Unity player name shown with ranked scores.

When online rankings are available, the game queues eligible results only for the Play Games account it knows when you finish a run. It sends a board identifier and your score or all-star completion time to Unity Leaderboards under that account. Other players can see your ranking, Unity player name and result. Pending results from earlier builds that lack an account stay on your device. Unity may deliver account notices. The app keeps a local record of notices you read.

Unity Leaderboards processes player identifiers, IP addresses and approximate location. Google Play Games processes game identity and collects SDK analytics and diagnostics for service reliability and improvement. The game’s cosmetic shop uses earned gold. For provider practices see Google’s Privacy Policy and Unity’s Player Privacy Policy.

How long data stays

Your local progress stays until you clear it or remove the app, subject to Android backup. In a build with online rankings, the game removes a submitted result from the queue after the service acknowledges it. Online profile details and rankings can remain with the services while the profile or entry exists. SquidSoft uses support correspondence to handle and verify requests. A provider may retain limited records under its own security, legal and backup policies.

Request account or data deletion

You can request deletion of your Gravity Ball online account and its associated ranking data even if you no longer have the game. Email SquidSoft at rameznoshy@gmail.com with the subject “Gravity Ball account and data deletion”. Include your Gravity Ball ranking name if you know it. You may also request deletion of ranking data alone. Never send a password, sign-in code or payment information.

SquidSoft will reply to verify account ownership and confirm the outcome. For a verified account deletion, SquidSoft requests removal of the game’s Unity Authentication profile and associated leaderboard entries. Your Google account and Play Games profile have separate controls through Google Account. Clearing local progress and removing a backup are separate actions you can take on your device.
